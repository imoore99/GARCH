## GARCH Model for S&P 500 Volatility Forecasting

## Advanced Risk Analytics & Volatility Modeling

PROJECT OVERVIEW:
Purpose: To measure the risk profile of the S&P 500 and determine volatility metrics for adequate risk management using advanced GARCH modeling techniques.
Objective: Determine the risk profile and forward-looking risk characteristics of the portfolio while assessing current market conditions to understand directional risk trends and regime changes.

BUSINESS PROBLEM:
Financial markets exhibit volatility clustering where periods of high volatility are followed by high volatility, and low volatility periods persist. Traditional models fail to capture these heteroskedastic patterns, leading to inadequate risk assessment and suboptimal portfolio management decisions. This project addresses the need for accurate volatility forecasting to support strategic investment decisions and risk management frameworks.

DATA SOURCES:
Primary: S&P 500 daily returns (historical period)
Secondary: Bond yields and gold prices for correlation analysis
Market Indicators: VIX and other volatility benchmarks
Frequency: Daily observations

KEY FINDINGS & RESULTS:
Directional Accuracy: 100% directional accuracy in volatility forecasting
Daily VaR: 2.17% to 3.51% under normal market conditions
Extreme Stress Testing: Potential daily losses of 7% to 12% during market crises
Dynamic Correlations: Bond-stock correlations become more negative during crisis periods, providing enhanced diversification benefits when needed most
Current Market Assessment: Identified elevated risk conditions in current market environment
